About me

Engineering and entrepreneurship

I'm a first-year mechanical engineering student, Zimbabwean by birth, and someone who learns fastest by building. Before I started my engineering degree, I ran businesses across agriculture, large format printing, insurance and art sales — which means I don't just see equations, I see the operational reality behind them: the costs, the constraints, the people and the messiness that no textbook captures.

That background shaped how I approach engineering. My default question isn't "what is the formula?" it's "what is actually broken here, and what is the simplest thing that fixes it?" I've applied that to food waste prediction in kitchens, predictive HVAC control in boardrooms, sludge valorisation in water treatment a housing platform for Zimbabwe and an automatic trenchcutting machine.

I'm currently deepening my multivariable calculus, pushing my C programming further, and getting hands on with PLCs and industrial automation because I want to move fluidly between the design desk and the plant floor.

Food Systems

PrePlate — Food Waste Prediction & Meal Pre-ordering

A platform tackling food waste at the source. It predicts how much food a kitchen should actually cook based on historical demand, occupancy and preorders — then lets customers preorder meals so kitchens cook to demand instead of guessing. Designed for schools, hotels and food outlets.

ZimRentTrust — Housing Agent Platform for Zimbabwe

A web platform for a Zimbabwean housing agent, built to make property listings, enquiries and tenant–landlord interactions transparent and trustworthy. Addresses a very local problem: unreliable rental information and friction between agents, landlords and tenants.

Materials

Water Treatment Sludge → Cement

A circular-economy materials project exploring how sludge from water treatment plants can be processed and valorised into cement — turning a disposal cost and environmental liability into a usable construction input.

Materials Engineering Circular Economy Water Treatment Sustainability

Control Systems

Predictive HVAC for Institutional Boardrooms

A predictive HVAC control concept for institutional boardrooms that forecasts occupancy and preconditions the space before people arrive. Combines feedforward control (acting on predicted occupancy) with feedback control (correcting against measured conditions) to cut energy waste without sacrificing comfort. I also added predictive maintenance to the HVAC.

Occupancy Prediction Feedforward + Feedback Energy Efficiency Thermodynamics

Where this is going

My goal: become an entrepreneur solving real African problems with mechanical engineering.

Africa doesn't have a shortage of problems — it has a shortage of engineers willing to stay close enough to those problems to solve them properly. I want to be one of the engineers who does. My long-term goal is to build and run engineering-driven ventures across the continent: companies that turn waste into materials, that cut energy waste in buildings, that make food systems smarter, and that use machines and automation to multiply what a small team can do.

Right now, that means building technical depth — mechanics, thermodynamics, fluids, design, controls and code — while keeping one foot firmly in the real world of customers, costs and operations.

Now

Master the fundamentals, ship real projects, get plant-floor exposure with PLCs.

Next

Internships and design work where mechanical engineering meets energy and food systems.

Later

Found and scale engineering ventures that solve problems across Africa.
